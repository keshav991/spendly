Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your email address when you create an account and the financial transactions you log within the application (amounts, categories, and dates).

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes generating your spending reports, maintaining your account, and improving the user experience of the Spendly application.

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to third parties. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or analytics on this platform.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through the support channels provided in your account settings.
